Where Is the Model Number on My Washer? Locations by Brand

By the Fixwise repair desk · Checked against each maker's support pages · Updated November 2026

Quick answer: On most top-load washers, lift the lid and look on the tub rim or under the lid near the hinge. On most front-load washers, open the door and look on the rim of the opening or on the inside of the door. If it isn't there, check the back of the washer or the back of the control panel. Use a phone flashlight, and take a photo so you have it for later.

Model number vs. serial number

The model number names the design (for example, a string that starts with WTW, GTW, WF or WM). Parts are matched to it. The serial number identifies your one machine and often encodes the build date. Warranty claims need both. Parts lookups need the model. Include every letter and digit, including any suffix after a dash or slash: it often changes which parts fit.

The maker's own support page for your washer type is the final word, because locations change between model years.

If the label is missing or worn

  1. Check your paperwork. The owner's manual, install guide, receipt or registration email often lists the model.
  2. Check the brand app. Connected washers (SmartThings, LG ThinQ, SmartHQ, Whirlpool app) show the model in device settings.
  3. Look behind the washer. Unplug it and turn off the taps before you pull it out. There is often a second sticker on the back.
  4. Ask the maker. With a serial number or proof of purchase, the maker's support team can often find the model for you.

Reading it right

  • Letter O and number 0 are easy to mix up, and so are I and 1. When in doubt, try both in the lookup.
  • Don't copy the "MFG date", "Type", voltage or "Rating" lines: they aren't the model.
  • Write the whole code exactly. A different revision suffix can mean a different pump or board.

FAQ

Is the model number on the washer's box or manual?

Often yes, but the sticker on the washer is the most reliable. Manuals usually cover several models.

Can I find parts with only the serial number?

Rarely by yourself. Contact the maker, who can usually look up the model from the serial.
