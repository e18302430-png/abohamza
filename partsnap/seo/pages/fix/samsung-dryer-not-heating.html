Samsung Dryer Not Heating: Codes, Free Fixes and Part Tests

By the PartSnap repair desk · Checked against Samsung support pages and parts data · Updated November 2026

Quick answer: If your Samsung dryer tumbles but stays cold, check power and the vent first. On electric models, a tripped half of the 240V breaker gives you a drum that turns with no heat. If power and airflow are fine, test the thermal fuse, heating element and high-limit thermostat, in that order. Gas models usually fail at the igniter.

Safety first. Unplug the dryer, or turn off the breaker, before you remove any panel. Electric dryers run on 240 volts. On gas dryers, shut the gas valve before you move the dryer. Never bypass a thermal fuse: it is a fire-safety device, and lint fires are a leading cause of dryer fires.

Free checks (do these first)

  1. Reset the breaker fully. Electric dryers use a double breaker. If one half trips, the motor runs but the element gets no power. Switch it fully off, then on.
  2. Clean the whole vent. Pull the dryer out and clean the duct all the way to the outside wall. Make sure the outside flap opens when the dryer runs. A clogged vent is the most common reason the thermal fuse blows, and it will blow again if you skip this step.
  3. Wash the lint screen with soap and water to remove dryer-sheet film.
  4. Gas model? Make sure the gas valve behind the dryer is open (handle in line with the pipe).
  5. Run a timed High-heat cycle. Sensor cycles can end early if the moisture bars are dirty, which can seem like no heat.

Confirm the part with a multimeter

Unplug the dryer. Take each part's wires off before you test it, so the meter reads only that part. Parts are reached through the back panel on many models and the front panel on others.

Gas dryers

  • Igniter: Look through the burner window at the start of a cycle. No orange glow points to the igniter (or the flame sensor or thermal fuse ahead of it).
  • Gas valve coils: If the igniter glows, then goes out with no flame, the coils are the usual cause.

FAQ

My Samsung dryer won't heat and won't start. Same problem?

On some models the thermal fuse also cuts the motor. If the drum won't turn at all, test the fuse and door switch first.

Can I reset a Samsung thermal fuse?

No. A blown thermal fuse is one-time and must be replaced, after you find out why it blew.

The dryer heats but shows HE.

That usually means it is overheating. Clean the vent, then test the high-limit thermostat and thermistor.
