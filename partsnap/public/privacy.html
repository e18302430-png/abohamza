PartSnap is operated by [Legal name / company], [Address]. Contact: [support email]. This policy covers PartSnap's website and app for users in the United States.

What we collect

  • Photos you choose to take (appliance labels, filter edges, problem areas). Your phone shrinks each photo and removes location data before it is sent. We send it to our AI provider, Anthropic, only to read the label or describe the problem. We do not keep your photos; a short anonymous fingerprint of the photo may be kept for up to 30 days so the same photo isn't processed twice.
  • What you type: model numbers and repair descriptions, used to answer your request.
  • Saved appliances and filter size: stored only in your browser on your device. Use "Delete my data on this device" to remove them.
  • Anonymous usage counts (for example "a label was scanned" or "a retailer link was clicked"), with no cookies, names or device IDs.
  • Security data: your IP address is used briefly to limit abuse and, if enabled, by Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from bots.

Affiliate links and cookies

When you click a retailer link (for example Amazon, eBay, PartSelect, Filterbuy, Sears Home Services, Angi or Thumbtack), that site may set its own cookies to credit us with a commission. Their privacy policies apply on their sites. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information, we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we don't send you email or text messages.

Your rights

Residents of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut and other states can ask what we hold and ask us to delete it by emailing [support email]. Because we keep almost nothing server-side, most data lives only on your device and you can delete it yourself. We won't treat you differently for asking.

Children

PartSnap is not directed to children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Service providers

Cloudflare (hosting, security), Anthropic (AI processing). Both process data on our behalf under their terms.
