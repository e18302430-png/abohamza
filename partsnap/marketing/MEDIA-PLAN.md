# Paid media plan (from the media-buyer review)

**Verdict:** paid traffic loses money until the funnel produces leads, not just scans. A click is worth about $0.12 when the value is counted per scan. Lead-gen pays more per conversion: a "book a pro" referral is worth about $14 per submitted lead. So paid budget goes to symptom searches that carry repair intent.

## Break-even

| Stream | Value | Click → conversion | Break-even CPC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scan | $0.30 | 40% | $0.12 |
| Parts order | $3 | 3% | $0.09 |
| Filterbuy subscription | $10–20 | 1% | $0.10–0.20 |
| Sears Home Services lead | ~$14 | 8–12% | $1.10–1.70 |
| Angi/Thumbtack lead | ~$8 | 5–8% | $0.25–1.20 |

## Channel order
1. Organic: SEO pages and 1–2 short videos a day, from week 1.
2. Google Search on long-tail brand + symptom searches. Max CPC $1.20. Never "near me".
3. Pinterest keyword targeting.
4. YouTube Shorts retargeting.
5. Reddit: helpful organic answers only.
6. Retargeting, once there are 1,000+ visitors a month.
7. TikTok Spark Ads, only on posts with over 3% engagement. CPC cap $0.40.

## 90 days (total cap $2,800)

| Weeks | Budget per week | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | $0 | Tracking, 30 videos, SEO pages |
| 3–6 | $150 | Google $90, Pinterest $40, TikTok Spark $20 |
| 7–10 | $250 | Move budget to winners |
| 11–13 | up to $400 | Only if 14-day ROAS is at least 1.2 |

## Kill and scale rules
| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Keyword | Kill at $30 spent with 0 leads, or $45 spent with cost per lead over $20 |
| Creative | Kill if the 2-second view rate is under 25%, or CTR is under 0.8% after 2,000 impressions |
| Channel | Kill if 30-day ROAS is under 0.5 after $300 spent |
| Scale | +20% when 7-day ROAS is at least 1.3 with 10 or more conversions, at most once every 72 hours |

The Ads Agent (`agents/media-buyer`) enforces these rules, spends at most $60 a day and $1,600 a month, and only proposes changes for its first 14 days.
