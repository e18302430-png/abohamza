Questions

Before you commission.

Is the preview the final portrait?

No. The preview shows your own photo painted in oil, so you can see the feel. The final portrait is painted by our studio in the role you choose, with the costume, background and lighting of that style.

How do I pay?

Checkout runs on Gumroad, a secure payment platform used by millions of creators. You can pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayPal, and sales tax is calculated automatically.

When do I send my pet's photo?

Right after payment. Your receipt has a link to upload the photo. The photo you use for the free preview stays on your device.

Will it really look like my pet?

Yes. We match the coat color, markings and face from your photo. You get one free revision, and a full refund if you are still not happy.

Is it a print or a file?

A high-resolution digital file you can print at home, at a local print shop, or order on canvas online.

Can I order for Christmas?

Yes. Nothing ships, so a portrait ordered before the countdown ends arrives before Christmas morning.
