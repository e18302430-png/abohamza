Questions

Before you commission.

What kind of photo works best?

A sharp photo in daylight, taken at their eye level, with both eyes and ears visible. Phone photos are perfect.

Will it really look like my pet?

Yes. We match the coat color, markings and face from your photo. If something is off, you get one free revision, and a full refund if you are still not happy.

Is it a print or a file?

A high-resolution digital file. Print it at home, at a local print shop, or order a canvas online. It is sized for prints up to 24 × 30 inches.

Can I order for Christmas?

Yes. Nothing ships, so a portrait ordered before the countdown ends arrives before Christmas morning. Add rush delivery for 12 hours.

Can you paint two pets together?

Yes. Choose "Two pets" in the commission desk and upload a photo of each after checkout.
